Carbon Cap & Trade Game

preparation

Multiplayer classroom cap-and-trade carbon market simulation

Carbon Cap & Trade Game is a real-time multiplayer cap-and-trade carbon simulation for the classroom. Students run companies in an industry they pick at join (a ten-year emission history is generated for that industry); the instructor allocates emission allowances under a cap mechanism and steps the class through game years: cap stage (regulator sale) → emission reveal → student market → penalty settlement.

In preparation — announced, not yet published. This page marks that the game exists; the hosted instance and downloads will be added once it’s deployed.
